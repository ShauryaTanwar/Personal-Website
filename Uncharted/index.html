    About Uncharted

    Explore freely or follow Atlas’s clues. Every discovery opens a little more of the world.

    Atlas and the area overviews are AI-generated and may make mistakes. Weather is model-derived current conditions from Open-Meteo, not a live station measurement.

    Map search: Photon with Open-Meteo / GeoNames city fallback, and © OpenStreetMap contributors. Earth and cloud imagery: NASA, via the WebGL Earth example. Globe: Three.js.

    Only recent chat and discovery names are sent to the guide through the app’s backend. Search terms and coordinates go to the map and weather providers. There are no accounts. Clue sessions expire after an hour or a backend restart.
