Privacy.

A short explanation of the data used by this personal portfolio.

Website visitors

This starter portfolio does not intentionally collect or store visitor names, email addresses, browsing histories, or Spotify account information. The contact form validates text in your browser and opens your email application rather than storing a submission on this site.

Spotify integration

If the Spotify module is enabled, the site uses Shaurya Tanwar's authorized Spotify account to request the single most recently played track. The server-side integration requests track metadata such as the track name, artist, album, artwork, Spotify link, and play timestamp so that the portfolio can display that information publicly.

The site does not ask visitors to sign into Spotify. Spotify client credentials and refresh tokens are stored as private hosting environment variables and are not intentionally sent to site visitors.

Third-party services

The Spotify module communicates with Spotify's Web API and displays Spotify-provided metadata and artwork. Use of Spotify is subject to Spotify's own terms and privacy practices.

Changes

If analytics, hosted contact forms, additional APIs, or other data-collecting services are added later, update this page before publishing those changes.
